# A forced binary orbit of internal source clocks

2026-10-20. **UNREVIEWED proposed all-parameter result.** This relates
consecutive gateway generations to their selected canonical sources.
It does not control b/c interruptions or establish global EXT.

## 1. Scope of the indexed segment

Use a valid all-a train 5,m_0,5,m_1,5,... as in CLOCK_FILTERED_TRAIN.
Choose a consecutive portion for which all triples used below have
catapult labels >=L>=17, and each source being normalized is internal
(followed by another catapult). ENTRY gives k_i=6 mod 16. Put

    u_i=(k_i+2)/8, an odd positive integer.

The exact TRAIN congruence gives

    4-k_i+3*k_(i+1)=8*(3*u_(i+1)-u_i)=0 mod 2^(L-2),
    u_(i+1)=3^(-1)*u_i mod 2^(L-5).               (CLOCK-ORBIT)

Thus the sources cannot be selected independently at successive
denominator generations. After t such transitions,
u_t=3^(-t)*u_0 modulo that same power of two.

Boundary caution: a finite train's last catapult need not satisfy
ENTRY, and the triple leading to it might have a smaller lower bound.
For a train with s labels, the universal L=17 conclusion covers
u_0 through u_(s-3), using triples entirely among the internal labels.
Do not apply it automatically to the last two catapult labels.

## 2. The return period is exponential in L

The order of 3 modulo 2^a is 2^(a-2) for a>=3 (the repeated-squaring
valuation proof is given in ORBIT_SHIFT_OBSTRUCTION). Since u_0 is
odd, the forced clock orbit has exact period

    p_L=2^(L-7).

Before p_L transitions its normalized clock residues are distinct.
If the same actual catapult parameter occurs twice within the
covered portion, its k and u are identical, so their position
difference must be a multiple of p_L.

In particular L=17 gives p_L=1024. The same source parameter
cannot recur at any positive distance below 1024 inside such a
portion. Each intervening return to five costs two loss units, so
such a recurrence would consume at least 2048 units of loss.
This does not bound a sequence of distinct labels or a trajectory
that leaves the covered all-a segment.

## 3. An entire residue period changes the next clock bit

Across p_L consecutive internal returns, the physical clock increases by

    Delta=sum_i(12+6*k_i)=48*sum_i u_i.

Let a=L-5. The p_L residues of u_i modulo 2^a form one coset of
the subgroup generated by 3. It is precisely the residues 1 or 3
modulo 8, or the residues 5 or 7 modulo 8. Writing N=2^(a-3), the
sum of the least positive residues in either coset is respectively

    4*N*(2*N-1), or 4*N*(2*N+1).

Both have valuation a-1. Higher lifts of each u_i add multiples of
2^a and cannot change that valuation. Therefore, exactly,

    v2(Delta)=4+(a-1)=L-2.                        (ADVANCE)

The clocks agree modulo 2^(L-2) after one period but disagree at
the next bit. This is an obstruction not recorded by a graph that
tracks only the normalized clock residue.

## 4. One residue period cannot be an exactly repeated source pattern

Suppose a length-p_L catapult pattern were repeated with enough
following context for the pair constraints to apply throughout.
The first occurrence of a pair (m_i,m_(i+1)) requires

    Theta_i=-21-9*k_i mod 2^e_i,
    e_i=min(m_i-2,m_(i+1)).

The second occurrence requires the same residue, so 2^e_i must
divide Delta. Some label in the period is strictly greater than L:
otherwise all clocks would be identical, contrary to the distinct
u residues and p_L>1. At that position e_i>=L-1, while ADVANCE
gives v2(Delta)=L-2. Contradiction.

This also rules out exactly repeated patterns with an odd number
of residue periods: each period's advance has valuation L-2, and
an odd sum still does. It does not rule out all even multiples,
nonperiodic source lists, interrupted histories, or inter-scale escape.
